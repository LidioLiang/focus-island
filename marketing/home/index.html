FAQ

Before you settle in.

What is Focus Isle?

An immersive workspace with calm surroundings, a focus timer, and ambient sound.

How do I start a session?

Open the app, choose a space and a seat, then set your timer. The timer preview on this page does not save a session.

Can I change my surroundings?

Yes. Explore café, library, and forest settings. Try the scene previews above.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is needed to use the web app. Open Focus Isle in your browser and begin.

Which timer modes can I use?

Choose a 25-minute or 50-minute countdown, or use free timing to count up. Pause and resume whenever you need.

Can I control the background sound?

Yes. Adjust music and ambient sound separately. Sound starts when your focus timer starts and pauses when you pause or end the session.

Can I stop the scenes from changing automatically?

Yes. Open Scene and turn off Auto-switch. You can still choose a view yourself or swipe between views in the app.
