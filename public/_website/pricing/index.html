Already a customer?

Existing purchases and entitlements remain managed in the app. Making basic café sessions free does not automatically cancel an existing subscription. Open your account’s billing settings to review renewal or cancellation.

What does the free website timer include?

The standalone timer offers 25-minute and 50-minute focus modes and a five-minute break, with three scene previews. It does not save sessions to your app account or play audio.
