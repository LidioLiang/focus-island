Give your next 25 minutes one job

Set a clear task, use a timer, and make a deliberate choice about what comes next.

Focus Isle editorial · 2 min read

In this articleDefine what the session is forSet a time boundaryKeep a place for distractionsPause when the timer endsChoose the next session intentionally

Define what the session is for

Choose one action: review a page of notes, outline a section, or explore one bug. If the task is large, identify the first part instead of expecting to finish the whole thing.

Set a time boundary

A 25-minute countdown provides a clear stopping point. You can use the free timer on this website or enter the full Focus Isle experience. If 25 minutes does not fit your day, choose a different duration.

Keep a place for distractions

When something unrelated comes to mind, write a brief reminder and return to your task. This is a practical suggestion, not a requirement to ignore urgent needs or push through discomfort.

Pause when the timer ends

Take a moment to notice what you did. Stand up or rest if that is what you need. The website timer also offers a five-minute break mode, but it will wait for you to start it.

Choose the next session intentionally

You may want another short session, a longer block, or to stop. A timer is a boundary you can adjust, not a score you need to achieve. Leave a short note about where to resume before you move on.

Try it in your own space.
