How to use this timer

Choose a scene and a duration. Press Start timer, then work on one task. Pause or reset whenever you need. When the time ends, choose your next focus or break session yourself.

Keep the tab open

The timer uses elapsed clock time to catch up after a background tab is delayed. Reloading or leaving the page resets this preview. No session history or sound is recorded or played here.
