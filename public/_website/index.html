What is Focus Isle?

An immersive focus app with café, library, and forest settings, seat selection, focus timers, and ambient sound. Open it in your browser to begin.

How does the website preview work?

You can switch scenes and use a timer here. Start focusing opens the full app for seat selection and audio controls. Preview sessions are not saved to your app history.

Is it free to try?

Basic café focus sessions are free to repeat and registration is optional, according to the current product terms. See the pricing page for the current offer and information about future plans.

Will choosing a scene here select it in the app?

The preview stays on this website. The full app opens at its normal entry point, where you choose your scene and seat.

Do I need to install anything?

The web experience opens in your browser. No app-store download is required to use it.
